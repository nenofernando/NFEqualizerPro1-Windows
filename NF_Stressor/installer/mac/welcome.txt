NF - Stressor v1.0.0
by NF Audio Tools

This installer places NF - Stressor into the standard system-wide plug-in folders so it's available to every audio application on this Mac.

You can choose to install the VST3 format, the Audio Unit (AU) format, or both.

A PDF manual (English and Portuguese) is also installed by this package, into /Users/Shared/NF Audio Tools/NF - Stressor/Manual.
