NF Mono Check v1.0.0
by NF Audio Tools

This installer places NF Mono Check into the standard system-wide plug-in folders so it's available to every audio application on this Mac.

You can choose to install the VST3 format, the Audio Unit (AU) format, or both.

NF Mono Check is a monitoring utility for your master bus: switch between Left, Mono, Right, and Stereo to check mono compatibility, phase cancellation, and stereo width, with a live correlation meter and goniometer.

A PDF manual (English and Portuguese) is also installed by this package, into /Users/Shared/NF Audio Tools/NF Mono Check/Manual.
